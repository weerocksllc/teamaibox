Team AI Box Trusted Media Service
==================================

Product description:
  AirPlay-compatible wireless casting (not Apple-certified)
  兼容 AirPlay 的无线投屏（非 Apple 认证）

FairPlay/DRM-protected content is unsupported.
不支持受 FairPlay/DRM 保护的内容。

Their license files and upstream notices are preserved in the corresponding

Runtime libraries
-----------------
The image uses Ubuntu 24.04 ARM64 packages, including GStreamer, Avahi,
OpenSSL and libplist. Package identities and license metadata are enumerated
in the image SPDX JSON SBOM. Distribution copyright files remain available
under /usr/share/doc in the image as supplied by Ubuntu.

Team AI Box modifications and corresponding source
----------------------------------------------------
Team AI Box does not modify the UxPlay source in this build. It supplies a
container build, a fixed runtime option boundary, a non-root supervisor and a
health probe. The complete build inputs and retrieval instructions are in:
  deploy/casting/TrustedMediaService.arm64.v2.Dockerfile
  deploy/casting/airplay-compatible-stack.lock.v2.json
  docs/operations/trusted-media-open-source-build.v2.md

No Apple logo, AirPlay badge, MFi badge or Apple/MFi certification claim is
included or authorized by this notice.
